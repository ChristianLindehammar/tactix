<en-US>
CoachMate 2.0.1
- Library exercises appear in your language when available, otherwise in English.
- Exercise diagrams explain what each arrow means, and the coach marker is back.
- Ice hockey uses puck and skating words.
- Tap a goal in the exercise editor to turn it.
- The keyboard closes when you scroll the exercise list.
