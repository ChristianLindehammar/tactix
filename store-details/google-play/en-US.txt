=== Full description (max 4000) ===
CoachMate is the team and tactics app for coaches in floorball, soccer, ice hockey, bandy and basketball. Plan your line-ups, draw your plays and build your training exercises in one place.

TEAM AND LINE-UPS
• Create as many teams as you need, with their own rosters
• Set line-ups on a realistic court or field and manage the bench with drag and drop
• Save several line-ups for different situations
• Paste a list of names to add a whole roster at once
• Send a team as a file for sharing or backup

TACTICS BOARD
• Draw plays with player and opponent markers, cones and a ball or puck
• Show runs and passes with solid, dashed and curved arrows
• Clear diagrams for team talks, half-time and training

COACH TOGETHER
• Invite other coaches to a team and plan it together
• Changes to players and formations reach everyone within seconds

EXERCISES (SUBSCRIPTION)
• Design training exercises in several steps, with curved arrows and slaloms
• Tag your exercises by sport, focus and difficulty, rate them and find them quickly
• Discover drills from other coaches in the shared library and copy them to your own
• Share your best exercises with other coaches
• Saved to your account and ready on your other devices

GETTING STARTED
• Teams and the tactics board work without an account, right on your device
• Sign in with Google or email for exercises and shared teams
• Available in English, Swedish and Finnish

SUBSCRIPTION
Exercises are part of CoachMate Premium, a monthly or yearly subscription with a 14-day free trial. Everything else in the app is included without a subscription.

The subscription is charged through Google Play when the free trial ends and renews automatically until you cancel. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
