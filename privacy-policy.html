Privacy Policy for CoachMate

Last updated: November 6, 2026

CoachMate is provided by Lindehammar Konsult AB, Sweden ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. This policy explains what we handle, why, who else receives it and what your rights are.

1. The Short Version

  • You can use CoachMate's teams and tactics without an account. They then stay on your device.
  • Exercises and sharing teams with other coaches need an account. We then store your email address, and what you save is uploaded to your account.
  • Exercises you save are public by default, so other coaches can find them in the exercise library. You can make an exercise private before saving it.
  • Other coaches never see your email address, only a display name if you choose one.
  • Paid plans are handled by Apple or Google, and we never see your payment details.
  • CoachMate has no advertising and no analytics, and we do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

Your teams, players and tactics are saved on your device. They are not sent to us unless you share a team with other coaches (see section 4). If you export or share a file from the app, it goes to whoever you choose to send it to.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

4. What Other Coaches Can See

Public exercises can be found and opened by every coach signed in to CoachMate, together with your display name if you have one. Other coaches can copy them into their own exercises. Private exercises do not appear in the library, but anyone signed in who has an exercise's link can open it.

Shared teams: when you share a team, its name, sport, players' names and positions, and formations are stored on our server, so that every coach in the team can see and change them. The coaches in a team see each other's display names. Anyone you give an invite link can join the team for 7 days, or until it is cancelled.

Please do not write personal details about players in exercises, and only add the player details your team needs, such as first names, to shared teams. When you add players to a shared team, you are responsible for being allowed to share their details with the other coaches.

Reports: any coach can report a public exercise they find objectionable. A reported exercise is hidden from the coach who reported it, and if several coaches report it, it is hidden from everyone until we have reviewed it. We receive the exercise, its title and the reason given, and may remove the exercise. The coach who made it is not told who reported it.

5. Paid Plans

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google takes your payment under its own terms, and we never receive your card details.

We use RevenueCat to check which plans are active. RevenueCat receives a random identifier for you, your purchase history, and technical details such as your device type, app version and IP address. If you sign in, it also receives your CoachMate account number, but not your email address. RevenueCat is used even if you have no account.

7. How Long We Keep Data

  • Your account and exercises: until you delete your account.
  • A single exercise you delete: it disappears from your lists, the library and links, but stays on our server, marked as deleted, until you delete your account.
  • A shared team: until the coach who shared it stops sharing it or deletes their account. Each coach keeps their own copy on their device.
  • Team invite links: they work for 7 days.
  • Reports and blocks: until the account of the coach who reported or blocked, or of the coach who made the exercise, is deleted.
  • Sign-in codes: they work for 10 minutes and are then removed.
  • Limits against abuse: hashed counts are removed when the limit period ends, usually within an hour.
  • Server logs and backups: up to 30 days.

8. Deleting Your Account

Open Settings, then Account, and choose to delete your account. This immediately removes from our database your account, all your exercises, including public ones, the teams you shared, your memberships in other coaches' teams, your reports and your blocks. Everything disappears from our backups within 30 days. Exercises and teams on your device stay there.

Copies other coaches made of your public exercises belong to them and stay. Changes you made to a team another coach shared stay in that team, as they are part of it, but they are no longer linked to an account.

If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to remove CoachMate from your Apple ID.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings. Contact us if you also want your purchase data removed from RevenueCat.

9. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its use, and receive a copy in a portable format. Contact us to use these rights.

You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, imy.se), or to the data protection authority where you live.

10. Children

CoachMate is made for coaches and is not directed at children. Children under 13 should not create an account. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
